Hi, I'm Luiz.

Distinguished AI Scientist & Data Science Executive. Problem Solver. Driving AI Innovation.

I'm a Data Scientist and Entrepreneur with more than 20 years of experience in Artificial Intelligence. I hold a PhD and MSc in Computer Science, both focused on Natural Language Processing and Information Retrieval. My expertise spans Recommender Systems, Machine Learning, and Data Mining.

I'm an academic at heart with a strong commercially-focused mind. My postdoctoral research at the University of Sydney was applied directly at one of the largest online dating platforms in Australia, and led to the creation of Octosocial Labs, focused on friendship recommendations. I was also the first data scientist at 1-Page Ltd (San Francisco HR tech), working on employee recommendation and NLP problems for major financial institutions.

At Accenture Liquid Studio, I led expert teams solving hard problems — including machine learning systems for homomorphically encrypted data (PyHEAL). I'm now a senior AI leader at Commonwealth Bank of Australia, leading data science teams across all business areas — including the first Generative AI implementations like CommBank Gen.ai Studio, the award-winning detection of financial abuse using AI, and a weather data model for real-time risk.
