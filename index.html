About

I'm a Data Scientist and AI leader with over 20 years of experience building intelligent systems. Currently a Distinguished AI Scientist at Commonwealth Bank of Australia, where I lead teams delivering some of Australia's most impactful AI solutions.

My expertise spans Natural Language Processing, Recommender Systems, Machine Learning, and Generative AI. I'm an academic at heart with a commercially-focused mind — my postdoctoral research at the University of Sydney was directly applied at one of Australia's largest online dating platforms, and the principles behind that work led to the founding of Octosocial Labs.

At CBA, I led the development of the first Generative AI implementations including the CommBank Gen.ai Studio, the award-winning detection of abuse using AI in financial transactions, and a cutting-edge weather data model.

Before CBA, I consulted for large enterprises at Accenture Liquid Studio, where I led teams solving extraordinarily hard ML problems — including developing machine learning systems for homomorphically encrypted data, published as the open-source PyHEAL library.

Projects

🤖 AI · Open Source
LLMMe

A personal AI email assistant that learns to write exactly like you. Built by training an LLM on every message you ever composed, it connects to Gmail and auto-drafts replies — so you can review rather than write from scratch. Because the rate of emails had exceeded my capacity to respond.

GitHub Blog post

📱 iOS · On-Device AI
GenAICam

A privacy-focused iOS camera app powered by Apple's FastVLM. Describes what it sees and generates images from those descriptions — entirely on-device, no internet required. A vibe-coding experiment that became a CVPR-inspired demo.

GitHub Medium
↗
🔐 ML · Cryptography
PyHEAL

Open-source Python library that makes machine learning on homomorphically encrypted data accessible. Developed at Accenture Liquid Studio, enabling ML models to operate on encrypted data without ever decrypting it.

GitHub (Accenture) Blog post
↗
🌐 Social · RecSys
Octosocial Labs

A startup built on reciprocal recommender systems research — focusing on friendship and people recommendation. Spun out of postdoctoral research at the University of Sydney applied to real-world social platforms.
