Luiz Pizzato, PhD

Distinguished AI Scientist · Commonwealth Bank of Australia

I'm a Data Scientist and AI leader with over 20 years building intelligent systems. My work spans Natural Language Processing, Recommender Systems, and Generative AI — from academic research to production systems used by millions of people.

At CommBank I lead teams delivering some of Australia's most impactful AI — including the first Gen.ai Studio, award-winning abuse detection in financial transactions, and a novel weather data model. Previously at Accenture Liquid Studio, and before that a postdoc at the University of Sydney.

I hold a PhD and MSc in Computer Science, both focused on NLP and Information Retrieval. I'm an academic at heart with a commercially-focused mind.
