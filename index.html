About

AI leader & researcher

I'm a Data Scientist and entrepreneur with over 20 years of experience building intelligent systems. I hold a PhD and MSc in Computer Science, both focused on Natural Language Processing and Information Retrieval.

As a Distinguished AI Scientist at Commonwealth Bank of Australia, I lead multiple data science teams across all business areas of the bank — including the first Generative AI implementations like the CommBank Gen.ai Studio, the award-winning detection of financial abuse using AI, and a weather data model.

I'm an academic at heart with a commercially-focused mind. My postdoctoral research at the University of Sydney was directly applied at one of Australia's largest dating platforms, and the principles behind it led me to found Octosocial Labs, focused on friendship and people recommendation.

Earlier, I was the first member of the data science team at 1-Page Ltd (San Francisco HR tech) and led teams at Accenture Liquid Studio solving extraordinarily hard problems — including developing ML systems for homomorphically encrypted data, published as the open-source PyHEAL library.

Projects

Things I've built

✉️
LLMMe AI · Open Source

A personal AI email assistant trained on your own writing style. Connects to Gmail and auto-drafts replies so you review instead of compose — built after email volume exceeded my capacity to respond.

↗
📷
GenAICam iOS · On-Device AI

Privacy-focused iOS camera app powered by Apple's FastVLM. Describes what it sees and generates images — entirely on-device, no internet required. A CVPR-inspired vibe-coding experiment.

↗
🔐
PyHEAL ML · Cryptography

Open-source Python library for machine learning on homomorphically encrypted data. Developed at Accenture Liquid Studio — enabling models to run on encrypted data without ever decrypting it.

↗
🌐
Octosocial Labs Social · RecSys

Startup built on reciprocal recommender systems research — focusing on friendship and people recommendation. Spun out of postdoctoral work at the University of Sydney applied to real-world social platforms.
