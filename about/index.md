Luiz Pizzato is a Distinguished AI Scientist at [Commonwealth Bank of Australia](https://www.commbank.com.au/), with a PhD in Computer Science and over 20 years of experience in machine learning and AI. He is the pioneer of reciprocal recommender systems, a field he defined by developing the first algorithms for people-matching in online dating and talent recruitment, and has co-authored more than 50 peer-reviewed publications with over 1,700 citations.

At Commonwealth Bank, Luiz has led AI teams that delivered solutions protecting vulnerable customers from [financial abuse](https://www.afr.com/technology/cba-turns-to-ai-to-root-out-abusive-payments-20211009-p58ykg) [📃](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.08016) [💻](https://github.com/h2oai/AITD), supporting communities during [natural disasters](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/cba-runs-its-smart-data-model-during-mass-downpour-562494), and driving early [Generative AI adoption](https://gizmodo.com.au/2023/05/commbank-gen-ai-tool/) across the Group. His work contributed to CBA being ranked the #1 bank globally for Responsible AI in 2024.

Before CBA, Luiz held research positions at the University of Sydney, led innovation labs at Accenture, and worked with start-ups, consistently turning AI research into products, patents, and open-source contributions.
